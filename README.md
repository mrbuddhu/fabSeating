# Fab Seating

Fab Seating designs and makes furniture and furnishings for homes, offices, and hospitality spaces. The showroom is in Kilpauk, Chennai, and the company has been at work since 2001.

## Visit the website

The site is a place to look through the collections, open a catalogue, and reach the team.

- **Home** introduces the showroom, the furniture categories, and recent Google reviews.
- **Categories** such as sofas, beds, wardrobes, dining, curtains, and rugs each have their own page. A product card opens a short description and a WhatsApp enquiry.
- **Catalogues** can be viewed or downloaded, then followed with a question to the team.
- **Gallery** shows photographs from the studio.
- **Solutions** covers residential, office, and hospitality projects.
- **Journal** collects longer articles on choosing and furnishing a space.
- **Contact** holds the showroom address, phone number, email, and an enquiry form.
- **Policies** and **Legals** explain how enquiries, delivery, privacy, and website use are handled.

Call, WhatsApp, or the enquiry form all reach the same showroom team. Directions open the Kilpauk listing on Google Maps.

## Editing the site

Text, photographs, video, catalogues, and search titles are edited in Sanity Studio, then published. The live pages update from that publish. A field left empty keeps the wording already on the site.
