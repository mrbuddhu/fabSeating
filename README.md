## 🏗️ Architecture Overview

### Modern Stack
- **Next.js 14** - Full-stack React framework
- **TypeScript** - Type-safe development
- **Tailwind CSS** - Utility-first styling
- **Sanity CMS** - Headless content management
- **Resend** - Email delivery service

## 🚀 Core Features & Modules

### 1. Product Catalog
- **Product Listings** - Browse furniture by category
- **Product Details** - Comprehensive product information
- **Multiple Categories** - Office, residential, hospitality, and more
- **Product Variants** - Different options and configurations
- **Image Gallery** - High-quality product photos
- **Product Reviews** - Customer feedback and ratings

### 2. Content Management
- **Sanity CMS Integration** - Easy content updates
- **Blog & Articles** - Furniture tips and inspiration
- **Case Studies** - Project showcases
- **Team Section** - About the company
- **Dynamic Pages** - SEO-optimized content

### 3. Customer Features
- **Enquiry Forms** - Contact and product enquiries
- **Search & Filter** - Find products easily
- **Responsive Design** - Works on all devices
- **Fast Performance** - Optimized loading speeds
- **Accessibility** - WCAG 2.0 AA compliant

### 4. Analytics & Marketing
- **Google Analytics 4** - User behavior tracking
- **Google Ads** - Conversion tracking
- **Meta Pixel** - Facebook advertising
- **SEO Optimized** - Metadata and structured data
- **Performance Optimized** - ISR and image optimization
